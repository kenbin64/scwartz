Minimal material, maximum surface.

The Schwarz Diamond is a real minimal surface: the least material that still carries the most surface, and it holds itself up, the way a diamond lattice needs no outward prop, every level supporting the next. As a principle it is simple and useful: do the most with the least. Strip a thing to what is load-bearing, and what remains is both smaller and stronger.

The thinking →
Honest about the claim. The Schwarz Diamond is genuine minimal-surface geometry, real and well studied. Using it as a principle for storage, tokens, and wording is a way of seeing, a lens, not a claim that those things are literally that surface. It is not a speed trick and it does not make anything smarter. It does one plain thing: more from less, which on the bill looks like lower storage and fewer tokens, and in practice looks like work that holds itself up.
